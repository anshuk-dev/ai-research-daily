### 1. PhysLoop: Harnessing LLMs' Physics Awareness for Self-Improving Video Generation Models
**🏛️ Source:** `arXiv / Hugging Face (44 upvotes)` &bull; **🏷️ Pillar:** `Autonomous Agents & Self-Improvement`

> **The Crux (Simple English):**  
> Video generation models produce visually realistic videos but still struggle with physical dynamics and causal consistency. Learning from generated videos requires fine-grained physical supervision, yet reliable quality judgments, correction pairs, and controlled variations are difficult to obtain.

**Key Takeaways & Paradigm Shift:**
  - 💡 **Core Innovation:** Video generation models produce visually realistic videos but still struggle with physical dynamics and causal consistency.
  - 📊 **Key Results:** We release 100K videos spanning diverse, realistic indoor and outdoor scenes, along with generation tools and two evaluation datasets: PhysLoop-Gen and PhysLoop-Repair.
  - 🚀 **Why It Matters:** We evaluate controllable generation and localized repair on these datasets, respectively, and assess physical behavior on the external Physics-IQ Verified benchmark.

### 2. G-STAR: Group-Scaled Token Capacity for Deeply Compressed Autoregressive Generation
**🏛️ Source:** `arXiv / Hugging Face (42 upvotes)` &bull; **🏷️ Pillar:** `Reasoning & Frontier Architectures`

> **The Crux (Simple English):**  
> Discrete autoregressive models offer a scalable framework for image generation. However, they face a fundamental trade-off: long token sequences preserve representation quality but slow generation, while aggressive spatial compression reduces representation capacity.

**Key Takeaways & Paradigm Shift:**
  - 💡 **Core Innovation:** Discrete autoregressive models offer a scalable framework for image generation.
  - 📊 **Key Results:** Based on product quantization, we show that, under a fixed nominal information budget, group size should scale quadratically with the per-axis spatial compression factor.
  - 🚀 **Paradigm Shift:** Building on this principle, we propose GSTAR, a spatial--group co-scaling framework that enables \(32\times\) and even effective \(64\times\) compression while preserving strong reconstruction, understanding, and generation performance.

🔗 **Primary Source URL:** [https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.13004](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.13004) &bull; [📥 Download PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.13004.pdf)
