### 1. The Trinity:Capability-Driven Recursive Self-Improvement for Multimodal Agents
**🏛️ Source:** `arXiv / Hugging Face (533 upvotes)` &bull; **🏷️ Pillar:** `Autonomous Agents & Self-Improvement`

> **The Crux (Simple English):**  
> Multimodal intelligence can be viewed as human-guided co-evolution between worlds and agents through interaction. However, scaling this process faces two bottlenecks: constructing heterogeneous, verifiable environments is costly, and manual Harness iteration cannot keep pace with growing task diversity and complexity.

**Key Takeaways & Paradigm Shift:**
  - 💡 **Core Innovation:** We therefore introduce The Trinity, a recursive self-improvement framework coupling world generation with Harness evolution through three roles: Creator, Explorer, and Evolver.
  - 📊 **Key Results:** However, scaling this process faces two bottlenecks: constructing heterogeneous, verifiable environments is costly, and manual Harness iteration cannot keep pace with growing task diversity and complexity.
  - 🚀 **Paradigm Shift:** Drawing on verified execution trajectories, Evolver shifts the basis of task organization from surface categories to shared requirements, reorganizing the original task tree into a hierarchical capability tree.

### 2. Multiscale Policy Optimization for Long-Horizon LLM Agents
**🏛️ Source:** `arXiv / Hugging Face (373 upvotes)` &bull; **🏷️ Pillar:** `Autonomous Agents & Self-Improvement`

> **The Crux (Simple English):**  
> Long-horizon LLM agents interact with their environments through complete assistant turns while generating each action token by token. We observe that critic values vary primarily across turns, motivating optimization anchored at turns with finer credit allocation among tokens.

**Key Takeaways & Paradigm Shift:**
  - 💡 **Core Innovation:** We propose Multiscale Policy Optimization (MPO), combining Multiscale Advantage Estimation (MAE) and turn-level importance sampling to align credit assignment and policy updates at the turn scale.
  - 📊 **Key Results:** Extensive experiments across search, tool-use, and automation tasks with 4B and 30B-A3B models demonstrate consistent gains over strong RL baselines.
  - 🚀 **Paradigm Shift:** Extensive experiments across search, tool-use, and automation tasks with 4B and 30B-A3B models demonstrate consistent gains over strong RL baselines.
